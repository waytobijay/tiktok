Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how IT Gateway (“we”, “us”, or “our”), operated under the domain bistab.com.np, collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website and services (the “Service”).

We are committed to protecting your privacy. By using IT Gateway, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you sign in with TikTok Login Kit, we receive basic profile information such as your TikTok display name, profile picture, and unique user ID (scope: user.info.basic).
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the Service (pages visited, features used, timestamps).
  • Device & Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device type, and operating system.
  • Content You Share: Any videos or content you choose to publish to TikTok through our platform (scopes: video.publish and video.upload).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Service
  • Personalize your experience (e.g., showing your name and profile picture)
  • Enable TikTok login and content posting features
  • Communicate with you about updates or support
  • Analyze usage to improve our platform
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. TikTok Integration & Third Parties

When you use TikTok Login or Content Posting features, your data is also subject to TikTok’s Privacy Policy. We only request the minimum scopes necessary:

  • user.info.basic – to display your name and profile picture
  • video.publish and video.upload – to allow you to share videos to your own TikTok account

We never post content without your explicit permission. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Cookies and Tracking

We may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences and understand how the Service is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to provide the Service and fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.
